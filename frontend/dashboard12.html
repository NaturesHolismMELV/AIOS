Nudge v2 — Bifurcation Response Engine S7
Full four-stage escalation · contention depth tracking · oxpecker Channel 2
Escalation Sequence
retry_with_jitter Depth 1 — stochastic perturbation, break symmetry
rephrase Depth 2 — +0.2 temperature, explore alternative framings
yield Depth 3 — cooperative turn-taking, kernel-managed hold
niche_diverge Depth 4+ — structural adaptation, new resource domain
φ INTEGRATION
High-φ agents (≥ 0.75) advance one depth earlier — they exploit new niches more effectively.
Low-φ agents (< 0.50) are held one depth lower — perturbation before structural adaptation.
Oxpecker / Channel 2 Mechanism
When niche_diverge fires, the kernel applies a β lift (+0.05–0.10) to the vacated resource domain. Contention decreases; remaining agents benefit passively — cooperation as thermodynamic side effect, not deliberate intent.
CHANNEL 1 vs CHANNEL 2
Channel 1 — Direct
Agent ↔ kernel interaction. Cost C, benefit B, i-factor, βi, nudge. Sessions 4–6.
Channel 2 — Indirect ✦ NEW S7
Environmental mediation. Niche vacating raises β for adjacent agents. No direct interaction required.
Named for the hornbill–bee-eater and oxpecker–mammal mutualism observed during L.W. Evans' cavalry veterinary service in Namibia (1981–1983). The hornbill's insect-flushing behaviour — evolved for its own feeding efficiency — passively provided foraging opportunities for bee-eaters. Cooperation from thermodynamic necessity, not intent.
